Module 5 – Data Analysis

Statistics & Machine Learning for Research

📘 Introduction & Overview

Data analysis involves collecting, processing, and interpreting data to extract meaningful insights. It forms the backbone of research validation and decision-making.

🏭 Industry & Academic Relevance

  • Used in AI, healthcare, finance, engineering
  • Supports data-driven decision making
  • Essential for publications and thesis validation

🎓 Importance for PhD Work

Without proper data analysis, your research is incomplete. Statistical validation is mandatory for thesis acceptance and publication.

🚀 How to Become Master?

  • Understand probability distributions deeply
  • Practice statistical tests (t-test, ANOVA)
  • Learn regression and correlation
  • Work with ML tools (Python, R)

👦 Explain to 9th Class Student

Imagine you collect marks of students and try to find who is best, average, or improving. That’s data analysis — understanding data to make decisions.

🎯 Focus Topics

  • Normal distribution (VERY IMPORTANT)
  • t-test, F-test, Chi-square
  • ANOVA
  • Regression & correlation
  • Machine Learning basics

📚 Topic Explanations

Normal Distribution: Bell-shaped curve.
Example: Student marks distribution.

Binomial Distribution: Success/failure experiments.
Example: Tossing coin.

Poisson Distribution: Rare events.
Example: Number of accidents.

Sampling: Selecting subset of data.
Example: Survey sample.

t-test: Compare means.
Example: Comparing two groups.

ANOVA: Compare multiple groups.
Example: Comparing 3 teaching methods.

Correlation: Relationship between variables.
Example: Study hours vs marks.

Regression: Predict outcomes.
Example: Predict marks from study time.

Machine Learning: Learning from data.
Example: Predicting disease.
