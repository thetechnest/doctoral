Unit 1 – Research Methodology 🚀

🌟 Why This Unit is Important
This unit builds the foundation of research thinking. If you master these topics, you can design research, write papers, and solve real-world problems effectively.
1. Research Definition & Objectives
Research is a systematic investigation to generate knowledge. Objectives include discovery, validation, and problem-solving.
2. Knowledge Economy
Modern economies depend on knowledge, innovation, and intellectual skills rather than physical labor.
3. Types of Research
Basic (knowledge), Applied (solution), Qualitative (opinions), Quantitative (data-driven).
4. Critical & Creative Thinking
Critical thinking analyzes problems logically; creative thinking generates innovative ideas.
5. Research Problem & Question
Identifying a clear research problem and forming precise research questions is essential for direction.
6. Research Design & Good Research
A good research design ensures validity, reliability, and systematic approach.
7. Hypothesis (Formulation & Testing)
A hypothesis is a testable assumption that is verified through experiments or data analysis.
8. Literature Review & Search Methods
Includes keyword search, backward and forward search to identify existing work.
9. ICT Tools & Databases
Tools like Google Scholar, IEEE, Scopus help in accessing research data efficiently.
10. Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)
Includes patents, copyrights, trademarks protecting research outputs.
