RPE Unit 5 – Publication Misconduct

Ethics, Integrity & Tools for Ph.D Scholars

📘 What is this Unit?

This unit focuses on ethical issues in research and publication, including fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, authorship disputes, and tools used to detect misconduct.

🎯 Why should you study this?

  • To protect your academic reputation
  • To avoid serious penalties including PhD cancellation
  • To understand ethical research practices
  • To publish with integrity

🎓 Importance for Ph.D Completion

Even a single case of plagiarism or misconduct can lead to rejection of thesis, blacklisting, or cancellation of degree. This unit is CRITICAL.

🚀 How to Become Expert?

  • Always write original content
  • Use plagiarism tools before submission
  • Understand authorship rules
  • Study real fraud cases
  • Follow ethical guidelines strictly

⚠️ FFP (Fabrication, Falsification, Plagiarism)

Basic:
  • Fabrication: Making up data
  • Falsification: Manipulating results
  • Plagiarism: Copying others' work
Advanced:

These are considered serious research misconduct and can lead to permanent academic ban.

Real-Time Example:

A researcher alters experimental results to match expected outcomes → this is falsification.

Focus Points:
  • Always maintain raw data
  • Use citations properly
  • Never manipulate results

👥 Authorship Issues

Basic:

Authorship should be given only to those who contributed significantly.

Advanced:

Includes ghost authorship, gift authorship, and disputes over contribution.

Real-Time Example:

A professor adds their name without contribution → unethical authorship.

Focus Points:
  • Follow ICMJE guidelines
  • Clear contribution roles

⚖️ Conflict of Interest

Basic:

Occurs when personal or financial interests influence research.

Advanced:

Can bias results, interpretation, and publication decisions.

Real-Time Example:

A researcher funded by a company publishes only positive results.

Focus Points:
  • Always disclose conflicts
  • Maintain transparency

📢 Complaints & Appeals

Basic:

Process to report and resolve research misconduct.

Advanced:

Handled by institutional ethics committees and regulatory bodies.

Real-Time Example:

A journal retracts a paper after plagiarism complaint.

Focus Points:
  • Follow institutional procedures
  • Maintain documentation

🧪 Plagiarism Detection Tools

Tools:
  • Turnitin
  • Urkund
  • Open-source tools
Basic:

Detect similarity in text.

Advanced:

Provide similarity reports, source matching, and percentage scores.

Real-Time Example:

You upload thesis → tool shows 18% similarity → you revise content.

Focus Points:
  • Keep similarity below acceptable limit
  • Paraphrase properly
