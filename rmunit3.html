Module 3 – Publication Ethics & Misconducts

Integrity is the Backbone of Research

📘 Introduction & Overview

Publication ethics ensures honesty, transparency, and integrity in research. Misconduct damages credibility and can lead to serious academic consequences.

🏭 Industry & Academic Relevance

  • Ensures trustworthy research outputs
  • Maintains scientific credibility
  • Required for journal publications and funding

🎓 Importance for PhD Work

Even small ethical violations can lead to rejection of thesis, blacklisting, or cancellation of PhD.

🚀 How to Become Master?

  • Understand FFP clearly
  • Use plagiarism tools regularly
  • Follow COPE and WAME guidelines
  • Maintain research transparency

👦 Explain to 9th Class Student

Imagine copying homework from a friend and submitting as your own. That’s wrong. In research, doing this is called plagiarism and is very serious.

📚 Topic Explanations

Ethics: Moral principles in research.
Example: Honest data reporting.

FFP: Fabrication, falsification, plagiarism.
Example: Changing results.

Redundant Publication: Same work published multiple times.

Selective Reporting: Showing only positive results.

COPE/WAME: Ethical guidelines for publishing.

Conflict of Interest: Bias due to personal gain.

Predatory Journals: Fake journals with no review.
