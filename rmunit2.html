🎯 5 Marks Questions & Answers
1. Explain project proposal structure.
A project proposal includes title, objectives, methodology, budget, and expected outcomes. It is prepared to secure funding from agencies by clearly presenting research goals and feasibility.
2. Explain research report writing.
Research reports present findings in a structured format including introduction, methodology, results, and conclusion. Clear writing ensures effective communication of research outcomes.
3. Explain types of conference presentations.
Oral presentations involve direct speaking, while poster presentations use visuals. Oral allows detailed explanation; posters enable quick interaction.
4. Explain referencing styles and tools.
Referencing ensures proper citation of sources. Styles include APA, IEEE, MLA. Tools like Zotero and Mendeley help manage references efficiently.
5. Explain thesis structure and evaluation.
A thesis includes preliminary pages, main content, and references. Evaluation involves examiner reports and oral defense to assess research quality.
