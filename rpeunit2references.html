Research Ethics – Zero to Hero 🚀

🌟 Why This Topic Matters
Ethics is the soul of research. Without ethics, even correct results lose value. Ethical researchers build trust, credibility, and long-term impact, while unethical practices destroy careers instantly.
📘 FFP (Falsification, Fabrication, Plagiarism)
  • Falsification: Changing data
  • Fabrication: Creating fake data
  • Plagiarism: Copying without credit
Fake Data
→
Wrong Results
→
Loss of Trust
🎤 “FFP doesn’t just break rules… it breaks your research identity.”
📘 Redundant Publications
Explanation:
Publishing the same work multiple times or dividing one study into many small papers (salami slicing).
One Study
→
Multiple Papers
→
Misleading Output
🎤 “Quality of research matters more than quantity of papers.”
📘 Selective Reporting
Explanation:
Showing only positive results and hiding negative outcomes.
All Data
→
Filtered Data
→
Biased Result
🎤 “Truth in research means showing what worked… and what failed.”
📘 Research Integrity
Explanation:
Following honesty, transparency, and responsibility throughout research.
  • Accurate data
  • Proper citation
  • No manipulation
🎤 “Integrity is what you do when nobody is watching your research.”
📘 Ethical Principles
  • Honesty
  • Objectivity
  • Transparency
  • Respect
🎤 “Ethics is not a chapter in research… it is the foundation of every chapter.”
🧠 Final Summary
✔ FFP → avoid misconduct
✔ Redundant → avoid duplication
✔ Selective → avoid bias
✔ Integrity → maintain trust
✔ Ethics → guide behavior

👉 Master ethics = Become a respected researcher.
