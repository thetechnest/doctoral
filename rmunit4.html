Module 4 – Design of Experiments & Optimization

Smart Experimentation for PhD Research

📘 Introduction & Overview

Design of Experiments (DOE) is a systematic method to plan, conduct, and analyze experiments efficiently. Optimization methods help find the best conditions for desired outcomes.

🏭 Industry & Academic Relevance

  • Used in manufacturing, AI, pharma, engineering
  • Reduces cost and improves efficiency
  • Essential for data-driven research

🎓 Importance for PhD Work

DOE helps you design experiments properly, reduce trials, and get accurate results — which is critical for thesis quality and publication.

🚀 How to Become Master?

  • Understand design types (CRD, RBD, Latin Square)
  • Practice Taguchi & RSM problems
  • Learn optimization techniques
  • Use software (MATLAB, Minitab)

👦 Explain to 9th Class Student

Imagine testing which fertilizer grows plants best. Instead of random guessing, you plan tests smartly to get best result quickly. That’s DOE!

🎯 Focus Topics

  • Factorial designs (VERY IMPORTANT)
  • Taguchi method
  • Response Surface Method
  • Optimization algorithms

📚 Topic Explanations

Completely Randomized Design: Random allocation of treatments.
Example: Testing medicines randomly.

Randomized Block Design: Removes variability.
Example: Testing crops in different fields.

Factorial Design: Multiple factors tested simultaneously.
Example: Temperature + pressure together.

Taguchi Method: Uses orthogonal arrays for optimization.
Example: Optimizing engine performance.

Response Surface Method: Models relationship between variables.
Example: Finding best combination of inputs.

Optimization Algorithms: Genetic, swarm methods.
Example: AI finding best solution.
