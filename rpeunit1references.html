Research Fundamentals – Zero to Hero 🚀

🌟 Why You Must Learn This
These concepts form the foundation of research. If you master them, you can design, analyze, and present research like a professional. Without them, research becomes directionless and weak.
📘 Types of Research
Explanation:
Research is classified into different types based on purpose and method:
  • Basic Research: Expands knowledge
  • Applied Research: Solves real-world problems
  • Qualitative: Focus on opinions
  • Quantitative: Focus on numerical data
Flow:
Problem
→
Research Type
→
Solution
🎤 “Choosing the right type of research is half of the problem solved.”
📘 Hypothesis Formulation
Explanation:
A hypothesis is a testable statement predicting a relationship between variables. Steps:
Observation
→
Question
→
Hypothesis
→
Testing
🎤 “A strong hypothesis turns confusion into direction.”
📘 Literature Search Methods
Explanation:
Literature search helps in understanding existing research.
  • Keyword Search
  • Backward Search (references)
  • Forward Search (citations)
🎤 “If you don’t read what exists, you will repeat what already exists.”
📘 ICT Tools
Explanation:
ICT tools help in accessing and analyzing research data.
  • Google Scholar
  • IEEE Xplore
  • Scopus
🎤 “Smart researchers don’t work harder… they work smarter using tools.”
📘 Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)
Explanation:
IPR protects your research work.
  • Patent: Invention
  • Copyright: Content
  • Trademark: Brand
🎤 “If you don’t protect your idea, someone else will own it.”
🧠 Final Summary
✔ Research type → defines direction
✔ Hypothesis → defines focus
✔ Literature → builds foundation
✔ ICT → improves efficiency
✔ IPR → protects innovation

👉 Master these = You are no longer a beginner.
